About

A legacy spanning generations

St. Paul H.S. School began in 1956 as the boys' section of St. Raphael's H.S. School near Sat Prachar Press. Sr. Ann served as principal and Fr. J. Partch was in-charge.

In 1963, Fr. Wilhelm J. Lenzen became the first Principal after the boys' section was separated from St. Raphael's with its own recognition, identity and name.

As the school developed, the secondary section moved to the new Boundary Road campus in 1975. The school was affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education, Delhi in 1996.

A school tradition built through education, leadership, commitment and generations of students.

Nursery Admission for Boys & Girls

  1. Registration forms for Session 2026–27 were available from 24 November 2025 to 29 November 2025 on working days from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
  2. Age criterion stated for Nursery: children born on or before 31 July 2023.
  3. Offline forms and prospectus were available from the High School Campus (Primary Wing) on payment of ₹500.
  4. Online registration was also made available through the school website.
  5. A passport-size photograph of the child was required.
  6. A photocopy of the child's birth certificate was required.
  7. Registered candidates and parents were to be called for certificate verification.

Documents Mentioned in the Notice

Child passport-size photograph, birth certificate, Aadhaar cards of the child, father and mother, Samagra ID with correct names and applicable caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC).
